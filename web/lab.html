Recipe lab

Change a generator and try the result right here, before anything is published. Everything runs in your browser; your edits are kept as a draft on this device.

1 Describe the change

Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or similar. It contains the recipe, the format rules and your current settings. Or edit the recipe below yourself.

2 Paste the AI's answer

The recipe is taken from the answer, checked, and loaded into the preview.

3 Check it and publish

Try the settings in the preview and look at Changes. When it looks right:

GitHub opens the recipe file with the new version on your clipboard: select everything, paste, then Commit changes / Propose changes. That creates a pull request; once it is merged, the website updates within minutes. Needs a free GitHub account.
