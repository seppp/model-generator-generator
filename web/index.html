{{head}} Print generators {{header}}

Parametric 3D print generators

Pick a generator, change the settings and download STL, multi-color 3MF for PrusaSlicer or an Onshape FeatureScript. Everything is computed in your browser.

Add your own

Every generator here is produced from a short JSON recipe: parameters plus a list of blocks (shape, QR code, text) that are added to or cut from colored parts. Try one in the Recipe lab, then save it as recipes/<id>.json in the repository.
