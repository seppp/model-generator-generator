Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

Who runs this site

Generator Generator is a private, non-commercial hobby project. It has no ads, no tracking and no income, so no legal notice (Impressum) is required. For any privacy question or request, open an issue at github.com/seppp/model-generator-generator/issues.

What data is processed

  • Hosting by GitHub Pages. When you open the site, GitHub receives your IP address, the time and the page requested, and keeps them in its server logs for delivery and security. Provider: GitHub, Inc. (USA). See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Everything else runs in your browser. The models, previews and downloads are made on your computer. The page only loads its own files (code, fonts, generators) from GitHub Pages. There is no server of ours, no account, no cookies, no analytics and nothing from other sites.
  • Your settings (for example the text or link in a QR code) are kept after the # in the page address. Browsers don't send that part to the server.
  • Share links carry the whole generator, compressed, after the # as well, so nothing is stored on a server. Anyone you give the link to can see that generator and your settings.
  • Local storage in your browser holds your saved generators (My generators), unsaved edits, a font file you choose, the theme and the live update switch. It never leaves your browser. Remove it by deleting your generators on the site or by clearing the site data in your browser.
  • Submit to the site opens a form on GitHub filled in with your generator, its share link (with your current settings) and your description. GitHub receives this when the form opens. If you create the issue, it is public and posted under your GitHub account, and GitHub's privacy statement applies.
  • Other services you choose to use. The AI prompt is only copied to your clipboard. If you paste it into an AI chat, or paste the FeatureScript into Onshape, that provider's privacy policy applies. Links to GitHub (How it works, Recipe format) work like any GitHub page.

Purpose and legal basis

Hosting logs serve the secure delivery of the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest). Local storage is only used for features you ask for and is strictly necessary for them (§ 25(2) TDDDG). Submissions are processed to review your generator (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Transfers to the USA

GitHub may process data in the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long data is kept

Data in your browser stays until you delete it. GitHub keeps its logs according to its own deletion periods. Submitted issues stay on GitHub until they are deleted; ask in an issue if you want yours removed.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). Send requests to the contact above.
